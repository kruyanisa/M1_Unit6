📌 Alaska Context:
Alaska is in the north-west corner of North America. Temperatures in Alaska in the summer vary from 16°C to 27°C. Early mornings and night time are from 4°C to 10°C. Late August and September are cool. Winters are cold with lots of snow. If you decide to visit "The Great Land", make sure you pack the right clothes.
